# Tam & Co Backups — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Tam & Co Ltd uses this private backup tool to protect its
job-management database and support recovery if data is lost.

## Data handled

Database backups may contain customer and staff details,
job and quote records, and account authentication records.
Backup files are confidential.

## Google Drive access

The tool uses Google Drive permission to create and manage
files and folders authorised for it. It uses this access to
upload, list, check and retrieve backup files.

It does not require access to Gmail or Google Contacts.

## Services used

Supabase holds the source database. The planned automated
backup process uses GitHub Actions to run backups and rclone
to transfer them to a designated Google Drive account.

These providers process data as needed to deliver their services.

## How data is used

Tam & Co Ltd uses backup data for business continuity and
recovery, not for advertising, sale to third parties, or
training AI models.

Access is limited to authorised administrators and the
services needed to operate the backup process.

## Retention and deletion

Backup copies are retained according to Tam & Co Ltd’s
business recovery and record-keeping requirements.
Authorised administrators manage their deletion.

Disconnecting the tool through Google Account permissions
stops future authorised access but does not delete existing
backup files.
