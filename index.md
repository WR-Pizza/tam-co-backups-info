# Tam & Co Backups

Tam & Co Backups is a private backup tool used by Tam & Co Ltd
to protect the data in its job-management app.

It saves database backups to a designated Google Drive account
to support recovery if data is lost. Daily and monthly automatic
backups are being configured.

This tool is for authorised Tam & Co staff only. Customers do
not need to connect a Google account or use it.
